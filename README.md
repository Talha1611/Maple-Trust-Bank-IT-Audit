# Maple Trust Bank — Simulated ISO/IEC 27002:2022 IT Audit Portfolio

> **Educational simulation.** Maple Trust Bank is fictional. All dates, identities, ServiceNow tickets, supplier names, findings and evidence are invented. This is **not** a completed audit of a real bank and does not provide an audit opinion, ISO certification or regulatory compliance assurance.

## Project overview

Designed a risk-based IT audit for a fictional Canadian bank with approximately 2,500 simulated employees, covering **January 1–June 30, 2026**. Used ISO/IEC 27002:2022 as security control guidance and separately identified **fictional bank policy requirements** for testing criteria.

## Scope — 16 controls

| Area | Controls | Topics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access Management | AC-01–AC-04 | Provisioning, termination, privileged access, periodic access reviews |
| Change Management | CM-01–CM-04 | Authorization, testing, segregation of duties, emergency changes |
| IT Operations | OP-01–OP-04 | Vulnerabilities, logging/monitoring, backups, restoration testing |
| Supplier & Application Security | SA-01–SA-04 | Supplier onboarding, supplier monitoring, secure development, application testing |

## Audit methodology

1. Defined scope, risks, control objectives, owners and audit period.
2. Created four Risk and Control Matrices (RCMs) with ISO mappings, expected evidence and proposed test steps.
3. Evaluated fictional ServiceNow, Active Directory, security, backup and supplier evidence excerpts.
4. Drafted one detailed workpaper for **AC-01 User Provisioning**; operating effectiveness remains **inconclusive** due to missing sample-level evidence.
5. Consolidated preliminary observations and prepared a qualified executive summary with limitations.

## What the evidence supports — and does not support

- **AC-02:** Two of 15 *simulated* terminations are classified as confirmed late-disablement exceptions **within the fictional sample**. This does not establish population-wide control effectiveness.
- **AC-01:** Three of 15 samples are flagged as possible late approvals; only U002 and U004 have specific timestamps. The third is unsubstantiated; the other 12 have no independently validated individual records.
- **Other controls:** RCMs contain illustrative records and **potential** exceptions. Full operating-effectiveness testing was **not completed**.
- **Overall:** No assurance opinion, control effectiveness rating, regulatory conclusion or claim of a real audit is made.

## Standards and copyright

ISO/IEC 27002:2022 is referenced by control number and paraphrased topic only. The licensed full-text standard is **not redistributed** in this repository. This project does not assert that ISO/IEC 27002 is itself a certification standard.


## Formal simulated audit opinion

The executive report includes a **disclaimer of opinion** in formal audit-report format. The 16-control environment was not tested sufficiently to support a qualified, unqualified, or adverse effectiveness opinion. This is a portfolio simulation, not a real independent assurance engagement.
